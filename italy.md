## ⚠️ Important Tips

* **St. Peter's Basilica** is free to enter, but lines are long unless part of a tour with direct entry from the Sistine Chapel.
* **Scam Alert**: Entry to the Basilica is free—avoid scammers who say otherwise.
* **Book early**: Reseller tickets often sell out quickly.
* **Cancellation**: Many providers offer free cancellation up to 24 hours in advance.
