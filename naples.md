## 🚆 How to Travel from Naples to Pompeii Scavi–Villa dei Misteri by Train

### 1. **Starting Point: Napoli Piazza Garibaldi Station**

* **Location:** This station is situated beneath the main Napoli Centrale station.
* **Access:** Follow the signs for "Circumvesuviana" or "Linea Napoli–Sorrento" to reach the Circumvesuviana platforms.([violetamatei.com][1])

### 2. **Purchasing Tickets**

* **Ticket Options:**

  * **Standard Circumvesuviana Train:** Tickets cost approximately €3.20 for a one-way journey.
  * **Campania Express (Tourist Train):** Offers air-conditioned coaches and guaranteed seating for €15 one-way or €25 round-trip. ([thebettervacation.com][2])

* **Where to Buy:**

  * At ticket counters or vending machines within the station.
  * For the Campania Express, tickets can also be purchased online through the official [EAV website](https://www.eavsrl.it/web/content/campania-express-2025) or authorized ticketing platforms.([pompeii-ticket.com][3], [thebettervacation.com][2])

### 3. **Boarding the Train**

* **Destination:** Ensure you're boarding a train heading towards **Sorrento**.
* **Departure Platform:** Trains typically depart from **Platform 3**. Note that there are no elevators or escalators to this platform, so be prepared for stairs.&#x20;
* **Frequency:** Trains run every 20–30 minutes, with increased frequency during peak hours.([pompeii-ticket.com][3], [visitpompeiivesuvius.com][4])

### 4. **Journey Details**

* **Travel Time:** Approximately 35–40 minutes on the standard train. The Campania Express takes about 30 minutes.
* **Arrival Station:** Disembark at **Pompei Scavi–Villa dei Misteri** station, which is the closest stop to the archaeological site. ([booking.visitpompeiivesuvius.com][5], [en.wikipedia.org][6])

### 5. **Reaching the Pompeii Archaeological Site**

* **Walking Distance:** The main entrance to the ruins, **Porta Marina**, is just a 5-minute walk from the station.
* **Facilities:** You'll find restrooms, cafés, and ticket booths en route to the entrance.([thebettervacation.com][2])

## 📝 Additional Tips

* **Seating:** The standard Circumvesuviana trains do not have reserved seating and can be crowded during peak times. If traveling with elderly parents, consider the Campania Express for a more comfortable experience.
* **Accessibility:** Be aware that access to Platform 3 requires stairs; plan accordingly if mobility is a concern.
* **Return Trip:** Trains back to Naples run frequently throughout the day. It's advisable to check the latest schedules for your return journey.([visitpompeiivesuvius.com][7], [thebettervacation.com][2])

## 🗓️ 4-Day Naples Itinerary (with Elderly Parents)

* **Morning:**

  * Take the **Montesanto Funicular** to reach **Vomero Hill**. This funicular connects the Montesanto area to the Vomero district, offering a comfortable ride up the hill.&#x20;
  * Visit **Castel Sant'Elmo** for panoramic views of the Bay of Naples. The castle is accessible and provides stunning vistas.([naplesitaly.ca][1])

* **Afternoon:**

  * Explore the **Certosa di San Martino**, a former monastery with beautiful architecture and art.
  * Enjoy lunch at a local café in the Vomero district.

* **Evening:**

  * Return to your accommodation and rest.

### **Day 2: Shopping & Local Markets**

* **Morning:**

  * Visit **Via Toledo**, a bustling shopping street with a mix of high-street brands and local boutiques.
