## 🏞️ Day Trip to the Amalfi Coast from Naples (No Car Required)

### 🚢 Option 1: Ferry from Naples to Amalfi

**Morning:**

* **Departure:** Take the ferry from **Naples Beverello Port** to **Amalfi**.

  * **Ferry Operators:** NLG (Navigazione Libera del Golfo) and Positano Jet.
  * **Duration:** Approximately 1 hour and 50 minutes.
  * **Frequency:** Up to 5 departures per day during summer months.
  * **Cost:** Starting from €28 per person. ([ferryhopper.com][1])

* **Arrival in Amalfi:** Disembark at **Amalfi Port**, located in the town center.

**Midday:**

* **Lunch:** Enjoy a meal at a seaside restaurant with views of the Mediterranean.

**Afternoon:**

* **Explore Amalfi:**

  * Visit the **Amalfi Cathedral (Duomo di Amalfi)**, accessible via a gentle incline.
  * Stroll through **Piazza del Duomo** and browse local shops.
  * Relax at **Marina Grande Beach** or enjoy a gelato by the promenade.

**Evening:**

* **Return to Naples:** Board the ferry back to **Naples Beverello Port**.

  * **Departure Times:** Multiple options available; check schedules in advance.
  * **Duration:** Approximately 1 hour and 50 minutes.([rome2rio.com][2])

### 🚌 Option 2: Bus from Naples to Amalfi

**Morning:**

* **Departure:** Take the **SITA bus** from **Naples Varco Immacolatella** to **Amalfi**.

  * **Duration:** Approximately 1 hour and 50 minutes.
  * **Frequency:** Several departures daily.
  * **Cost:** Starting from €6 per person. ([omio.com][3], [directferries.com][4], [rome2rio.com][2])

* **Arrival in Amalfi:** The bus arrives at **Piazza Flavio Gioia**, centrally located.([checkmybus.com][5])

**Midday:**

* **Lunch:** Dine at a local trattoria offering traditional Campanian dishes.

**Afternoon:**

* **Explore Amalfi:**

  * Visit the **Paper Museum (Museo della Carta)** to learn about Amalfi's papermaking history.
  * Take a leisurely walk along the **Lungomare dei Cavalieri**.
  * Shop for handmade ceramics and limoncello in local boutiques.([checkmybus.com][5])

**Evening:**

* **Return to Naples:** Board the **SITA bus** back to **Naples Varco Immacolatella**.

  * **Departure Times:** Multiple options available; verify schedules ahead of time.
  * **Duration:** Approximately 1 hour and 50 minutes.([omio.com][3])

---

**Tips for a Comfortable Trip:**

* **Advance Booking:** Especially during peak seasons, book ferry or bus tickets in advance to secure seats.
* **Mobility Considerations:** Both transportation options are suitable for elderly travelers; however, ferries offer more space to move around.
* **Weather Check:** Monitor weather conditions, as ferry services may be affected by rough seas.
* **Packing Essentials:** Bring sun protection, water, and comfortable footwear for walking on cobblestone streets.

Feel free to choose the option that best suits your family's preferences and enjoy your day exploring the beautiful Amalfi Coast!
